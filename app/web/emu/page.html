Preset lab

Every factory sound, by engine. Pick one to put it on a track of the FM-1 above and hear a short phrase; the panel's knobs then shape it, and the list follows. It replaces that track's sound in your project, which the FM-1 autosaves like any change; hold HOME and press REC to undo it. Switch the FM-1 on to hear them; you can look and filter until then.

Arrow keys move through the list, Enter plays the sound. With the list focused, the computer keys Q to I and A to K play it too.

Night Garden

Press Space to play, or fill in some steps: every track grows its own plant and the plants play what the tracks play.

Buttons

  • SEQ the steps: on a drum track, a black key picks the sound and the white keys are steps 1–16.
  • SEQ held, play notes, let go, then press steps: the notes go on them.
  • HOME held is the shift key: REC undo, PLAY redo, white key 1–3 loads a project.
  • EDIT the sound, ENV envelope and filter, FX sends, LFO, ARP players, GLO mix and master.
  • PLAY start / stop. REC held while playing records; stopped, a tap starts step record.
  • SEL the page's list: copy, paste, weave, presets.
  • SELECT the page, ALGORITHM the track, PRESETS the next sound, KNOB 1–4 the values on the screen. SAVE saves.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Combinations with a mouse: Shift-click (or right-click) a button or key to hold it down, click again to let go. A step held this way takes its locks from the knobs.

Keyboard

  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Hold Esc for HOME (the shift key), / for SEQ (held: play the notes a step will take).
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Z / X OCT− / OCT+.
  • ↑ ↓ turn SELECT.
  • Tab moves through the knobs, buttons and keys; there Space or Enter presses the one you are on, and the arrow keys turn a knob.

Garden

The Garden button under the panel opens a night garden with a plant for each track. A plant plucks, blooms and scatters pollen when its track plays; its filter opens it, its sends haze and echo it, a mute wilts it. The patterns seed the garden, so every song grows its own; Regrow plants another. Calm, Trippy and Wild set how much the camera moves and how often it glitches (with reduced motion on, it stays Calm). Screen pops the FM-1's own screen out of the small panel: large, above the board, or huge, beside it. The garden only draws and listens: it never changes the sound. It needs WebGL 2.

Demo songs

The Demo songs box under the panel loads a song into the running FM-1, all made with SoundPlant's factory sounds. Load and play goes to the Song page and plays the song from its first slot (SEQ, HOME, SELECT, PLAY: the same buttons you can press yourself); Load only leaves it for you to look at. A song replaces all three projects and the user presets, so the page asks first, and Undo puts back what you had saved. It will not load while the FM-1 plays: stop it first. To see a song grow, load it, then open the Garden.

Preset lab

The Preset lab under the demo songs lists SoundPlant's factory presets, by engine, from the firmware's own table. Click one, or press Enter on it, and it goes on the track you pick (the one the FM-1 has selected to begin with) and plays a short phrase; Play again repeats it. With the list focused, Q to I and A to K play the sound, as they do on the panel, and KNOB 1 to 4 (or a click and drag on them) shape it: the lab shows what each knob is called and where it stands. Find a sound filters by name, engine, role or genre. The lab steps aside while the garden is open.

Saving

SAVE keeps your projects in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory. Factory reset forgets them. Save backup writes the projects and presets to a file (the backup page and tool of the FM-1 read it too); Load backup… puts one back. It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is SoundPlant running, at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz. The idea of running FM-1 firmware in the browser comes from Groove OS's emulator.
